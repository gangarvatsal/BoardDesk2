Notice format
Edit the notice wording

Change any wording below. Words in braces fill in automatically: {COMPANY} {REG_ADDRESS} {BODY} {MEETING_WORD} {MEETING_NO} {FY} {FY_SHORT} {DATE} {TIME} {VENUE} {EMAIL} {ADVANCE} {LINK} {MEMBERS} {CHAIR} {ANNEXURES} {SIGNATURE}. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. **bold**, *italic*. Start a paragraph with CENTER: or LEFT: to align it, and with VC:, NOT VC:, SHORT: or NOT SHORT: to show it only for that kind of meeting.
